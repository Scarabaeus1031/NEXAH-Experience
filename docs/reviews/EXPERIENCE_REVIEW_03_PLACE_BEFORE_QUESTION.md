# NEXAH Experience Review 03 — Place before Question

> **Status:** Superseded at the entrance-copy level by
> [Experience Review 04](EXPERIENCE_REVIEW_04_CHOOSE_A_PLACE.md). This document
> remains as a record of the preceding refinement.

## Decision

Home is the entrance hall of NEXAH. It welcomes the visitor into an existing
place before asking for attention, language or action. The question remains an
available entrance, but it is no longer the premise of the entire house.

## Improvements

### Replace the question-led hero with an editorial welcome

The hero now uses three existing review sentences:

> Every journey begins somewhere.
>
> Welcome to NEXAH.
>
> Choose where you would like to begin.

This is stronger because the first impression communicates arrival rather than
performance. No input, button, system name or architectural explanation
competes with the welcome.

### Place the existing entrances directly after the welcome

The first continuation presents only five established destinations:

- Visitor Guide
- Living Atlas
- Library
- Laboratory
- Begin Orientation

This is stronger because it makes the scope of the public experience visible
without turning Home into a catalog. Each entrance leads to a room that already
owns its content and responsibility.

### Make Orientation a conscious destination

Begin Orientation has the same visual status as the other entrances. It is not
the default interaction and is not described through ORION terminology.

This preserves the deterministic boundary while allowing visitors to decide
whether a question is how they want to begin.

### Strengthen atmosphere without adding decoration

The existing landscape field is slightly more visible, navy typography retains
the visual authority, gold remains limited to orientation markers, and the
entrances use paper, hairlines and space rather than card effects.

This improves depth and legibility while preserving the established design
language.

## Preserved

- the existing global navigation;
- every existing room and its ownership;
- the question and Threshold flow;
- the Library, Living Atlas and Laboratory as independent destinations;
- static delivery, explicit links and visible boundaries;
- the absence of chat, hidden inference and product marketing.

## Intentionally unchanged

- No room was added or renamed.
- No architectural responsibility moved to Home.
- No new recommendation, ranking or personalization exists.
- No ORION explanation appears in the entrance hall.
- No new copy was introduced for the hero; the selected language came from the
  review brief.

The refinement changes the order of attention, not the architecture.

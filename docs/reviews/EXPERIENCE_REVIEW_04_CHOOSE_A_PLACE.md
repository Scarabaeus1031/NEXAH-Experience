# NEXAH Experience Review 04 — From “Ask a Question” to “Choose a Place”

## Decision

Home keeps the philosophical question:

> What are you trying to understand?

It no longer implies that the answer belongs in an input field. The immediate
continuation is spatial and editorial:

> Choose where you would like to begin.

This sequence answers “Where am I?” before asking “Where would I like to go?”

## Clearly stronger improvements

### Keep the question, remove the prompt behavior

The question is a statement of purpose rather than an interface instruction.
It remains centered in the atmospheric hero with no field, button, example
prompt or simulated conversation.

**Rationale:** The sentence preserves NEXAH’s philosophical identity while the
absence of an input removes the chatbot and search mental models.

### Present five equal rooms

The established entrances now appear directly below the hero:

1. Visitor Guide — Start here if you are new.
2. Living Atlas — Explore relationships and maps.
3. Library — Browse publications.
4. Laboratory — See how observations become research.
5. Begin Orientation — Enter ORION. Bring one real question.

**Rationale:** The visitor can see the public shape of NEXAH and choose a
relevant entrance without first understanding its architecture.

### Make ORION intentional

Begin Orientation has equal visual weight and remains the only entrance that
begins an active orientation journey.

**Rationale:** ORION is preserved and elevated without becoming the default
behavior of Home.

### Strengthen spatial presence quietly

The deeper navy heading, warm gold room numbers, existing landscape field,
paper surfaces and section boundary provide hierarchy without adding imagery,
effects or product language.

**Rationale:** The entrance reads more clearly as an editorial hall while
remaining recognizably NEXAH.

## Preserved

- the existing typography and palette;
- the five established destinations and their ownership;
- the Threshold and ORION journey;
- static, explicit navigation;
- the Library, Living Atlas and Laboratory boundaries;
- the absence of chat, search, recommendations and hidden inference.

## Not changed

- No room, architecture concept or workflow was introduced.
- No responsibility moved to Home.
- No destination was ranked or personalized.
- No runtime, provider or ORION behavior changed.

The refinement changes the visitor’s first decision from typing to choosing a
place.

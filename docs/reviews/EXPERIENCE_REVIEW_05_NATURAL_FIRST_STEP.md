# NEXAH Experience Review 05 — Every Room Should Have a Natural First Step

## Decision

Each major room now begins with an invitation appropriate to its existing
responsibility. The intervention changes editorial order and emphasis only. It
does not add rooms, content authority, recommendations or runtime behavior.

## Home

**Question:** Where would you like to begin?

No change. Home already introduces NEXAH as a place and presents five equal
entrances.

## Library

**Question:** What would you like to read?

The complete Catalog remains intact and immediately reachable. Before it, a
small editorial threshold names two recorded works:

1. The Visitor’s Guide — a first orientation through NEXAH.
2. LIBRARYBOOK — an overview of the complete collection.
3. Complete Publication Catalog — direct access to every recorded work.

**Rationale:** New readers receive a legible first step while returning readers
retain direct Catalog access. No publication is hidden, ranked or inferred.

## Laboratory

**Question:** How did this work come into being?

The existing hierarchy is retained and clarified:

1. Research process
2. Laboratory and its primary Cartography Laboratory reading
3. Repository
4. Current work

**Rationale:** The room begins with process, then exposes the work, its living
source and its current state. No new Laboratory content was required.

## Living Atlas

**Question:** What would you like to connect?

The existing explicit route remains the first demonstration. Visual
collections now follow it as a more visible invitation into the landscape,
before the larger concept, operator, theme and publication registries.

**Rationale:** The Atlas begins through relationships and collections rather
than reading order. Every relation remains registry-owned and curated.

## Begin Orientation

**Question:** What are you trying to understand?

No change. The active question belongs only to the existing Orientation
Threshold. ORION remains an intentional destination.

## Preserved boundaries

- Home owns arrival and choice.
- Library owns publications and Catalog presentation.
- Living Atlas owns declared editorial relationships.
- Laboratory presents research without replacing the Repository.
- ORION owns deterministic orientation.
- The Human owns interpretation and continuation.

No search-first workflow, recommendation system, personalization, inference or
new navigation was introduced.

## Why this belongs in Experience

Rödelheim Observatory begins with an ordinary Human situation: one person,
one window, one local horizon and a moving sky. It then shows how a bounded
view can become a clock, a map, a record, an instrument and finally a test.

This makes the work a strong bridge between the three public NEXAH layers:

1. **Human and culture** — lived place, attention, wonder and the experience
   of looking from somewhere;
2. **design and instruments** — apertures, projections, clocks, plates,
   retained traces and interactive HTML models;
3. **research and laboratory** — declared models, bounded tests, negative
   results and explicit claim ceilings.

The Experience repository may curate the reading journey and public
presentation. It does not acquire authority over publication identity,
scientific evidence or Human interpretation.

## Source statement

> A local window into nested celestial motion.

The Observatory begins with one bounded view of the sky: a window, a horizon,
a clock and an observer. It traces how spin, orbit, precession, nutation and
drift become visible through changing light, phase, projection and time. It
does not reproduce the cosmos; it documents how a world in motion becomes
observable from one particular place.

> The sky is not one clock. It is a score of nested clocks.

## Candidate book promise

Rödelheim Observatory may become a guided visual book about how one bounded,
local view is transformed into comparable records without being mistaken for
the whole sky.

A concise working promise is:

> An ordinary window becomes an observatory when its position, direction,
> transformations and limits are made visible.

This would complement, not repeat, the A0 Foundation Book:

- **A0** explains how a representation becomes a testable question.
- **Rödelheim Observatory** lets that method unfold through one real place,
  several celestial clocks and a return to the observer.

## Claim and authority boundary

- Visual analogy is not astronomical evidence.
- A generated observer-plane model is not a measured sky record unless its
  acquisition and calibration are explicitly documented.
- Interactive coherence does not establish a physical mechanism.
- Negative and limiting results remain part of the public story when their
  protocol and boundary are clear.
- The observer position is retained as context; it is not promoted to a
  universal center.
- Scientific adoption remains with the Science Lab and its documented review
  process.

The most important laboratory sentence currently available is:

> The instrument worked. The baseline did not justify a stronger claim.

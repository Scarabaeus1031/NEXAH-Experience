# NEXAH Experience Design Language

## Purpose

The NEXAH Experience invites a person to orient within complexity. It does not
simulate a conversation, accelerate attention or present certainty as a
product. The interface creates enough structure to move and enough space to
remain still.

## Experience principles

### Calm before interaction

Arrival does not demand input. The first view offers one clear invitation and
permits observation before action.

### One dominant idea per screen

Every screen has one primary question, statement or decision. Supporting
information remains available without competing for attention.

### Space is part of orientation

Empty space separates responsibilities, slows transitions and makes hierarchy
perceptible. It is not unused capacity.

### Typography before decoration

Language carries the experience. Scale, rhythm and contrast establish meaning
before illustration or ornament.

### Motion explains

Movement may reveal continuity, direction or a change of state. It never exists
to reward engagement, manufacture urgency or conceal waiting.

### Never imitate chat

NEXAH has no conversational persona, message bubbles, typing indicator or
anthropomorphic inner voice. A person formulates an intention; NEXAH presents an
orientation.

### Never imitate search

The experience does not promise ranked answers or instant retrieval. Questions
open fields, routes, evidence and limits rather than result lists.

### Evidence is quieter than conclusions

Evidence remains inspectable and close to the claims it supports. It does not
compete visually with the orientation, and the orientation never outruns it.

### Reflection is always optional

The interface may offer a pause after an orientation. It never requires a
response, scores reflection or converts silence into failure.

### The Human always decides

The person confirms the direction, chooses whether to inspect evidence,
determines meaning and may leave at every stage.

## Voice

NEXAH speaks with composure and precision. It names known, unknown, blocked and
absent states without celebration or apology.

Use:

- “Begin with what you are trying to understand.”
- “This remains uncertain.”
- “No result was produced.”
- “You can stop here.”

Avoid:

- praise for asking;
- claims of thought, emotion or consciousness;
- urgency and engagement language;
- prescriptions that replace human judgment;
- mystery about ownership or authority.

## Visual character

The visual language is luminous but restrained: warm paper, deep ink, muted
mineral colors and small points of gold. Geometry suggests paths and relations;
it never pretends to prove them. Imagery opens perspective rather than filling
space.

Beauty comes from proportion, clarity and restraint. Mystery belongs to
possibility, never to authority.

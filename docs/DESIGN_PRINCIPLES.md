# Design Principles

1. **Orientation before information.** Reveal the field before adding detail.
2. **Structure before surface.** Visual hierarchy follows responsibility.
3. **Clarity over complexity.** Complexity may remain; interaction may not be confusing.
4. **Reveal, do not overwhelm.** Detail appears through deliberate inspection.
5. **Evidence before assertion.** A claim and its support remain distinguishable.
6. **Connect, do not collect.** Relationships matter more than volume.
7. **Respect the unknown.** Missing and uncertain information remain visible.
8. **Build for becoming.** A result may open a better question without forcing one.
9. **Serve the explorer.** The product does not optimize for retention or persuasion.
10. **Stay transparent.** Status, provenance and boundaries are never decorative details.
11. **Evolve with purpose.** New elements must strengthen the journey, not merely expand it.

## Interaction guardrails

- Every consequential transition requires a human action.
- A secondary route never competes with the primary decision.
- Error and blocked states receive the same care as successful states.
- Silence, back navigation and departure are complete outcomes.
- Mobile is not a compressed desktop; it preserves the same hierarchy with less density.
- Reduced motion preserves meaning without animation.

## Authority guardrails

- NEXAH opens the Orientation Space.
- ORION may later provide navigation, but is absent from Sprint 01 runtime.
- LYRA informs language principles, but no language agent exists here.
- The Library is represented by structured editorial data and remains an independent authority.
- LUCY is not implemented. Reflection belongs to the Human.

# First Orientation Journey

## Purpose

Sprint 05 turns the existing Experience, deterministic ORION report, Editorial
Library and Living Atlas into one continuous reading path. It does not add a new
system. It curates one route through systems whose authority remains separate.

The journey begins with curiosity and ends without requiring closure:

```text
Home
  → one question
  → deterministic orientation
  → visible boundary
  → The Cartography Laboratory
  → Observation
  → Observe
  → Attention
  → The Wonder Operator
  → quiet departure
```

## Journey philosophy

- One question is enough. The first visit offers no imitation of open search or
  chat.
- A boundary is part of an orientation, not a failed answer.
- Editorial material appears only after the deterministic report has ended.
- Atlas links expose registered relationships; they do not imply evidence or
  infer similarity.
- Reflection is optional. The visitor may continue, return or leave.
- Leaving is a valid final interaction.

## Transition philosophy

Each transition answers three quiet questions:

1. Where am I?
2. What is this place giving me?
3. Where can I continue?

The primary continuation is singular. A quiet departure remains available. The
journey marker identifies the current movement without turning the path into a
score or completion mechanic.

The registered editorial chain used by this journey is:

| From | Relationship | To |
|---|---|---|
| The Cartography Laboratory | book–concept | Observation |
| Observation | concept–operator | Observe |
| Observe | operator–theme | Attention |
| Attention | book–theme, traversed toward its publication | The Wonder Operator |

The Experience chooses this route editorially. It does not create or infer any
Atlas relation.

## Page responsibilities

### Home

Offers one real question and one clear beginning. It does not promise that
arbitrary text will be interpreted.

### Orientation

Presents the existing build-time ORION result faithfully. The report status,
route, evidence, provenance, validation and blockers remain inspectable. The
public reading layer makes the boundary legible without changing it.

### The Cartography Laboratory

Provides editorial depth after the report ends. It does not support, validate
or amend the ORION result.

### Observation, Observe and Attention

Reveal an explicit Atlas neighborhood in three movements: an idea, an editorial
gesture and a theme. Their pages continue to expose their complete registered
relationships beyond the curated route.

### The Wonder Operator

Returns the visitor to sustained reading. It is the final publication on the
curated path, not a conclusion.

### Departure

Creates a complete, calm ending. It saves nothing, asks for nothing and leaves
the original question open.

## Reading rhythm

The rhythm alternates between narrow and wide views:

- question — narrow;
- route — structured;
- boundary — precise;
- publication — spacious;
- Atlas entries — relational;
- publication — reflective;
- departure — quiet.

Detailed ORION fields remain available through inspection sections instead of
competing with the main reading line. Library and Atlas pages keep their normal
content; the journey adds one restrained continuation rather than a new page
mode.

## Exit strategy

Every curated continuation offers a quiet departure. The final departure page
contains no completion badge, account request, recommendation loop or retention
prompt. It names only what the visitor encountered and returns authority to the
visitor.

The path is complete when the visitor can leave with a more precise question,
not when the system claims to have finished the subject.

## Authority boundaries

- ORION remains the authority for the deterministic report.
- LYRA remains the source of the report explanation.
- The Library remains editorial and independent.
- The Atlas owns only explicit curated relationships.
- The Experience owns sequence, pacing and presentation.
- The Human owns reflection, continuation and departure.

No runtime, AI, search, account, personalization or new architectural contract
is introduced by this journey.

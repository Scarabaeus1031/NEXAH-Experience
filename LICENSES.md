# NEXAH-Experience Licensing Scope

Licenses follow material type and provenance rather than applying one
undifferentiated license to every artifact.

| Material | Default license | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software, tests, configuration and implementation code | [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) | Original NEXAH-Experience software unless a file states otherwise |
| Original documentation, specifications, research, books and visual material | [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DOCS.md) | Original NEXAH Experience material unless a file states otherwise |
| Third-party or source-derived material | Its stated source license | Not relicensed by NEXAH-Experience |
| Publication assets and generated catalog/report projections | Their authoritative source license and provenance | Presentation or generation does not change ownership or licensing |

Repository location, rendering, indexing and generation do not change a
component's license. Local notices and provenance records take precedence.

Contributions follow the license of the destination material. Contributors
must identify externally sourced material and confirm that they may submit it
under the applicable terms.
